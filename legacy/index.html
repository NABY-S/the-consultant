About us

Where ideas become solutions

The Consultant is a technology consulting and development firm based in Ghana. We work at the intersection of research, software engineering and education, helping people move from a rough idea to something built, documented and working.

Our work spans two main directions. First, we build: websites, software and digital systems for businesses across every industry — retail, education, health, agriculture, logistics and beyond — as well as final year and industrial attachment projects for students in IT and Computer Science, carried all the way from concept and research through development, documentation and implementation.

Second, we teach. We run monthly training courses in IT and Computer Science for learners at every stage — Junior High School (JHS1–JHS3), Senior High School (SHS1–SHS3), and university level — building the practical tech skills that classrooms often don't have room for.

Whether you're a student under deadline, a business that needs a digital presence, or a school looking to build tech capacity in your students, The Consultant brings the research, engineering and mentorship to get it done properly.
